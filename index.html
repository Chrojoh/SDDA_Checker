SDDA Title Tracker

To use this tracker, upload the DOG SCORES file downloaded from your SDDA account. You may also upload the optional DOG TITLES file to confirm SDDA-recorded titles and title dates. Either file can be in CSV or XLSX format. The tracker will show recorded or calculated titles, qualifying score combinations, calculated Gold scores, and criteria still needed for further titles.
